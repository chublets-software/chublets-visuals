The graphics and tables are built from model tables of the chublets Wikibase
(`chublets-wikibase-demo`, `wikibase-static`, `wikibase-federation`) and the
Starter Pack, vendored under `data/raw/`. Wikidata content is CC0; the
CodeMeta/Wikidata/DataCite crosswalk is CC BY 4.0 (ConnOSS crosswalk, v1.2.0,
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21935688), so attributing under CC BY 4.0 is
compatible with all of them. A graphic that needs another licence names it in
its `captions.yaml` entry.
